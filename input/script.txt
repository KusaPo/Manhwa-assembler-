Imagine being so corrupt the moment you put on a badge, you become the very evil you swore to stop. This girl just reported a gang attacking another girl in an alley, and the cop she trusted? Smiled like everything would be handled.
But the moment she walks away, her gut starts screaming. Something is off. Way off.
She doesn't know it yet, but the officers she just trusted are the rottenest piece of trash in the entire precinct.
The patrol car sits parked. The corrupt cop's expression drops the second she's out of sight.
He smirks and casually says, 'Well, time to clock out.' Yeah. He has zero intention of helping.
His rookie partner stares in disbelief. The alley is literally right in front of them.
Inside that alley, a gang of thugs is surrounding a defenseless girl, exactly like the citizen reported.
The female officer panics. 'We have to go in right now!' But the senior just shrugs.
He pouts like a bored teenager. 'Go in for what? There's too many of them. We'd just get wrecked.'
She begs to call backup. He waves it off. 'Those punks will leave when they get bored. Happens here all the time.' Peak garbage tier behavior.
Then suddenly, a voice cuts through the chaos like a blade. A different officer. One who actually showed up to do his job.
He demands the thugs let the girl go. The gang leader turns slowly, smoke curling from his cigarette, sizing up the newcomer.
The thug puffs straight into the officer's face and sneers, 'What if I don't feel like it?' Bro just signed his own warrant.
The MC doesn't even blink. No warning. No speech. Just pure violence.
One strike. Bone-crushing. Teeth shattering. The thug's brain reboots like a broken Wi-Fi signal.
The cigarette drops in slow motion as the boss thug's whole world tilts sideways.
It hits the wet pavement with a quiet hiss. The fight just started, and one tap from this officer ended round one.
The thug crumples, holding his mouth, eyes rolling. Record breaking response time on display.
Meanwhile, the two cops in the patrol car? Their jaws are on the floor. 'How was that so fast?'
The MC dusts off his hand like he just swatted a fly. 'If you don't like it, you take the beating. What else?'
The rest of the gang isn't having it. They crack their knuckles, cursing, ready to swarm him.
Big mistake. The MC doesn't even shift his stance.
The boss thug barely remembers his last hit before another fist arrives, faster than thought.
His face twists out of shape. Head snaps back like a cheap spring. Defeated, again.
Inside the patrol car, the female cop's eyes are saucers. This officer is on a completely different level.
In just a few moves, the entire gang hits the pavement. Punks scattered like bowling pins.
But the boss earns his title. Half conscious, mouth full of damage, he claws back up with murder in his eyes.
He reaches into his jacket. You know what comes next. Either a gun or a knife, and trash this low? It's always a folding blade.
He pulls the dagger. 'Damn you, mutt. Today I'll end you!'
The officer just sneers. 'End me? Have you ever actually ended anyone before?'
'Of course I have!' the thug snarls. The MC laughs softly. 'Nonsense. Your eyes give you away.'
Then he grabs the thug's wrist and pulls the blade straight toward his own heart, whispering like a demon in his ear.
'Then end me. Right now.' The thug freezes. Hand trembling. Cold sweat pouring.
'If you can't end me,' the MC's voice drops, 'then you will perish here.'
The dagger slips from shaking fingers and clatters to the ground. The thug, broken in soul and body, finally cracks.
Backing away in pure terror, he whimpers the question every villain asks too late. 'Who the hell are you?'
The MC reaches out, places a hand on his shoulder, and grins. 'I'm the one who catches and interrogates trash like you. I'm a police officer.'
He squeezes. Hard. Veins bulge. The fabric of the thug's shirt wrinkles under the pressure as the scream tears from his throat.
But to understand why this rookie cop has the aura of a stone-cold predator, we have to rewind.
One week earlier. Inchon Port. Empty. Quiet. A blue shipping container sits in the fading light.
A shadow approaches. No hesitation. He opens the container like he owns it.
Inside? A whole crew of assassins. Sharp weapons. Cold eyes. These aren't street thugs. These are professional eliminators.
The newcomer's alias is Bi Gwang. The legendary assassin. Top-tier operative of a shadow syndicate that turned children into killing machines.
The leader of this welcome party is Maple Leaf. He's wiping his sword and grinning like he's been waiting forever for this exact moment.
Bi Gwang steps inside. 'Quite the crowd.' His voice is calm. Too calm.
Maple Leaf smirks. 'Budda told us to work with you on this one. The final mission. Your retirement gift.'
But Bi Gwang isn't an idiot. The atmosphere reeks of betrayal.
Then it happens. From behind, an assassin lunges, swinging an iron chain to wrap around Bi Gwang's neck.
His reflexes are inhuman. He catches the chain mid air, spins, and slams an elbow straight into the attacker's throat.
The would-be assassin drops like a sack of potatoes. The chain is now Bi Gwang's.
Maple Leaf's grin widens. 'This is your dismissal ceremony, Bi Gwang. Budda wants you logged out.'
Big mistake giving Bi Gwang a weapon. His bare hands were already lethal. Now he's got a stage.
The first assassin charges. Bi Gwang loops the chain around his neck like a leash.
Then jumps up and slams his knee straight into the man's face. Brutal hit. Instant lights out.
Another rushes in with an axe, aiming straight for Bi Gwang's head.
The blade is centimeters away. He doesn't even blink. Just leans back, pivots on his right leg, and kicks the axeman's neck sideways. Done.
This gang only knew how to be numerous. Like leeches at a free buffet.
Bi Gwang flows through them. Wraps the chain around another attacker, slips under his blade, snags the other end.
He pulls. Hard. The chain snaps. Skin pales. The body drops, life drained.
Maple Leaf's smug grin completely evaporates. Watching his entire team get demolished in real time.
Bi Gwang grabs the last coughing thug, slams him to the floor, and locks eyes with the leader.
'Planning to handle me with just this weak prep?'
Maple Leaf curses under his breath and reaches into his shirt. And when assassins do that, you know exactly what's coming.
A pistol. Cold steel. He levels it at Bi Gwang's chest.
'I hate using guns. But Budda's order is to give you a complete corpse. And guns? They finish things.'
Bi Gwang's eyes go bloodshot. His whole life crashes into this moment. The lies. The missions. The promised freedom.
'Why?!' he roars. Maple Leaf only smiles. 'Goodbye.'
The trigger pulls. Bi Gwang charges like a man already perishing, hatred burning brighter than any wound.
A gunshot echoes. Damage sprays across the floor. Smoke curls toward a golden Buddhist statue in the distance.
Cut to a grand ancient temple. An old monk sits before the Buddha statue, four incense sticks burning.
Below the incense rests a memorial tablet. The name on it? Bi Gwang. They've already prepared his funeral rites.
The monk's phone buzzes. He answers calmly. 'Is it finished?'
But the voice on the other end isn't who he expected. It isn't Maple Leaf.
It's Bi Gwang. Holding Maple Leaf's phone over his lifeless body. 'You son of a glitch.'
The monk's eyes glint. This is Budda. The boss. The mastermind. The one who kidnapped children and forged them into weapons.
'I was just preparing your 49th day ritual,' Budda chuckles. 'How did you know to call your own memorial?'
'Open the door,' Bi Gwang growls. 'I'm already at your temple. If you don't come out, I'm coming in.'
He throws the phone, tightens his grip on his dagger, and stares down a fresh army of waiting assassins.
Budda calmly hangs up, gazing at the spirit tablet. The smile on his lips never wavers. This is exactly the trap he wanted.
A massive thug steps forward to block Bi Gwang's path. Body twice his size. Confidence at maximum.
Bi Gwang doesn't slow. 'Budda. The bastard who kidnapped kids and turned them into eliminators. Killing for money. That's Budda.'
The big man swings his axe with a roar. Bi Gwang flicks his dagger. One slash across the waist. The giant folds.
'Out of the way, idiots! Your turn!' He's bleeding rage, eyes red, slicing through them like a man with nothing left.
Every stab a memory. 'They took my body.' One assassin drops.
'My life.' Another throat opens. Damage paints the temple floor.
'My family.' Then his body suddenly stiffens. Cold spreads through his back.
A dagger. Plunged deep between his shoulder blades from behind.
He turns slowly. A woman stares back, eyes wide with her own fear. The traitor blade still in her grip.
Bi Gwang roars and charges her, eyes burning with the fury of a cornered beast. 'And now he wants to take my life too!'
He moves through them like a storm. Every assassin perishes. But Bi Gwang's body is shredded with wounds.
In front of him, the cold barrel of Budda's pistol. The old monk, calm. Untouched. Smiling.
'You promised me!' Bi Gwang drops to the floor, gritting through agony. 'You promised me a human life if I finished this mission!'
Budda nods. 'And I am giving you a human life, Bi Gwang.'
He leans close, that demonic grin spreading. 'The end of a human life is death. That is the human life.'
Bi Gwang bursts into laughter. Cursing everything. Every mission. Every betrayal. Every word out of that bald monster's mouth.
Behind Budda, the majestic Hwagak Temple looms. A monk pressing a gun to an assassin's head. The cruelest mercy.
Around him, the broken bodies of every fighter he just wiped out. Damage everywhere. The legendary assassin, finally cornered.
'Namo Amitabha,' Budda whispers, like he's blessing the very person he's about to delete.
The gunshot rings out. Bi Gwang slowly closes his eyes, swearing he'll take revenge even from the underworld.
Darkness. Silence. The legend of Bi Gwang ends here. Or does it?
Time passes. Who knows how long. Then a dim light slips into his vision.
Shadows sharpen. Faces come into focus. Three young police officers, leaning over him with worried eyes.
He sits up. Confused. Disoriented. They stare back equally confused. 'What's with this guy?'
'The doctor said he has temporary amnesia,' the female officer panics. 'Kim Kyeongsoo, calm down!'
Bi Gwang's brain glitches. 'Who is Kim Kyeongsoo?' The female officer blinks. 'It's you. Of course.'
Another rookie shoves a mirror in front of him. Wait. What?
And hands him a police ID. Ilsong Station. Officer Kim Kyeongsoo. Rookie patrolman.
But the soul behind those eyes? That's still Bi Gwang. The legendary eliminator. Reborn inside the body of a cop.
He stares at his reflection like he's looking at a stranger. Different jaw. Different eyes. Different life.
His hand trembles. The face in the mirror isn't his. The ID isn't his.
Apparently, this body's owner tried to log out. Tried to end himself. Wrote a suicide note. The rope snapped, and he'd been unconscious for a whole week.
And now the body has a new tenant. One soaked in the memories of a thousand eliminations.
The three rookies keep rambling about how lucky he is. About the senior officers who bullied him. About the names he wrote in that note.
But Bi Gwang isn't listening. His mind is racing through one thought only. Revenge.
Budda. Hwagak Temple. The shadow syndicate. They all think he's dead. They think the legend is buried.
But fate just handed the deadliest assassin alive a new identity. A badge. A loophole.
Law on one side. A volcano of vengeance on the other.
He stares back at his new face. 'Dead but awake.' Pale. Stunned. Still wrapping his head around the impossible.
'I... am a police officer?' He says it out loud. Tasting the words. Hating them. Maybe loving them too.
The legendary eliminator Bi Gwang. Betrayed by his own organization. Reborn as a rookie cop.
Now with the badge of justice in one hand and the soul of an assassin in the other, his bloodstained path of justice begins. And trust me, this is just the beginning.
